Advisory & consulting

AI, innovation and R&D advisory

Independent advice for founders, CTOs and innovation leaders working on AI, software and research programmes.

Advisory focus

Technical direction for AI and software initiatives, plus strategy and leadership for research and innovation programmes.

Two areas of engagement

01Main advisory area

AI & Innovation Advisory

For an organisation developing, evaluating or committing to an AI or software initiative.

Typical work

  • Architecture and technical direction
  • Roadmaps and product decisions
  • Independent review of plans and risks
  • Prototype-to-product strategy
  • Build, buy or partner decisions

Focused format

Technical Decision Session

A focused 90-minute working session, with limited pre-read and concise written recommendations.

Defined review

AI & Software Direction Review

A structured 7 to 10 day review, ending in a decision memo and executive discussion.

How engagements are arranged

Defined before work begins

Scope, responsibilities and fee are agreed in writing.

The initial enquiry establishes context and fit. If it makes sense to proceed, I will suggest a short introductory call. It is not a free technical review.

Decision sessions
Offered for a fixed fee.

Reviews and sprints
Fixed scope and fee, agreed once the inputs and boundaries are clear.

Ongoing advisory
A monthly retainer for reserved capacity and agreed responsibilities.

Delivery
Based in Luxembourg and working internationally, primarily remotely. In-person work can be arranged separately.
